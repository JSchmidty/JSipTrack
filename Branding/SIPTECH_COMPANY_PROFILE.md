## COMPANY MISSION

SipTech builds awareness tools for health decisions that are too important to leave to guesswork. We start with alcohol — because BAC affects driving safety, medication safety, and long-term health, and because the data that should inform those decisions has historically been unavailable to individuals at the moment they need it most.

We believe that giving people accurate, private, nonjudgmental information is itself a health intervention — and that an empowered person makes better decisions than a lectured one.

---

## COMPANY VALUES

### 1. Awareness Over Advice

We give people data. We do not tell them what to do with it. This is not a cop-out — it is a deeply held design principle. When people have accurate information without judgment attached, they make better decisions for themselves than any nudge or warning can achieve. SipTech products are measurement tools, not behavior-change tools. The measurement is the intervention.

**In practice:** Every product feature is evaluated by the question, "Does this give the user more accurate information about their own body?" If the answer is no, it doesn't ship.

---

### 2. Privacy as Architecture, Not Policy

Privacy is not a checkbox in SipTech's compliance workflow. It is a foundational technical constraint. We build products that are technically incapable of leaking private health data because the data never leaves the user's device in the first place. We do not collect data we don't need. We do not retain data we've already used. We do not monetize user behavior.

**In practice:** SipTrack stores all drink logs on-device using encrypted local storage. SipTech's servers do not receive BAC readings, drink logs, or body profile information. This is by design — and it means we cannot be subpoenaed for data we don't have.

---

### 3. Inclusive by Default

SipTech products are designed for the full spectrum of humanity — every age, every ethnicity, every gender, every relationship with alcohol. We do not design for an assumed majority and then attempt to accommodate others as an afterthought. We begin with the full range of users and design inward. This means our contrast standards are higher than required, our type is legible at large sizes, our language works for people in recovery and people at dinner parties, and our imagery reflects the world as it actually is.

**In practice:** SipTrack was validated against 25 user personas before v1.0 shipped. Every design decision — color, copy, iconography, motion — was evaluated against that full set.

---

### 4. Scientific Honesty

SipTech does not overstate what its products can do. Our BAC estimates are estimates — we say so, clearly, in every context where the number appears. We do not market SipTrack as a breathalyzer, a medical device, or a precision instrument. We use the Widmark formula because it is the best available non-invasive estimation method, and we are transparent about its limitations.

**In practice:** Every SipTrack BAC reading is accompanied by a plain-language disclaimer. We label confidence intervals honestly. We will never let marketing language overstate the precision of our estimates.

---

### 5. Long-Term Thinking

SipTech is building a health technology company that could last 30 years. That means: we do not sacrifice user trust for short-term growth metrics. We do not add features that compromise privacy to hit a conversion target. We do not partner with advertisers in the alcohol industry. We grow by being genuinely useful and by users recommending us to people who need us. The compounding value of user trust is worth more than any growth hack.

**In practice:** SipTech does not sell advertising. SipTech does not partner with alcohol brands. SipTech's business model is a direct subscription from users who find the product valuable. That alignment — user pays, user benefits — is the only business model consistent with our values.

### For Press Kit

SipTech Inc. is a health technology startup developing privacy-first personal health awareness tools. ## BRAND DO'S AND DON'TS FOR MEDIA

**DO:**
- Refer to the company as "SipTech" (not "Sip Tech," not "siptech")
- Refer to the app as "SipTrack" (one word, capital S and T)
- Describe SipTrack as "a BAC tracking app" or "an alcohol awareness app"
- Note that all data is stored on-device (it is a key differentiator)
- Reference the Widmark formula for BAC calculation

**DON'T:**
- Call SipTrack "a drinking app" (excludes recovery users)
- Call SipTrack "a sobriety app" (excludes active drinkers)
- Describe SipTrack as "helping people drink responsibly" (prescriptive framing)
- Call BAC estimates "readings" — they are estimates, not measurements
- Refer to SipTrack as a "breathalyzer app" — it uses a formula, not sensing technology
- Use the term "standard drinks" — it is statistically misunderstood and internationally inconsistent
