### FAQ Section

---

**Q1: How accurate is SipTrack's BAC estimate?**
SipTrack uses the Widmark formula, which is the same method used in medical and research contexts to estimate blood alcohol content. It factors in your body weight, biological sex, the drinks you log, their alcohol content, and how much time has passed. Real accuracy depends on how accurately you log your drinks. Factors like metabolism, food intake, hydration, and health conditions can cause your actual BAC to differ from the estimate. SipTrack will always tell you it's an estimate — not a certainty.

---

**Q2: Is my data private?**
Completely. SipTrack stores all drink logs and session history on your device using local encrypted storage. Nothing is sent to SipTech's servers. We have no way to see what you've logged. You can export your data or delete it entirely at any time.

---

**Q3: Is SipTrack for people who are trying to stop drinking?**
SipTrack is for anyone who wants to know their BAC — regardless of why. Recovery Mode is available for users who want a streak-focused view without seeing a BAC gauge. The app makes no assumptions about why you're tracking.

---

**Q4: What is BAC?**
BAC stands for Blood Alcohol Content. It's the percentage of alcohol in your bloodstream. A BAC of 0.08% is the legal limit for driving in most US states. A BAC of 0.00% means no alcohol is detected. SipTrack's dashboard shows your estimated BAC as a number with two decimal places.

---

**Q5: Does SipTrack work on Apple Watch?**
Yes. SipTrack includes Apple Watch complications that show your current estimated BAC and session status. You can also log drinks directly from your Apple Watch. The complication updates whenever a new drink is logged or your estimate changes.

---

**Q6: How does the medication alert feature work?**
In Settings, you can enter the names of any medications you take. SipTrack includes a database of 500+ medications with known alcohol interactions (SipTrack Plus feature). When your logged BAC is above 0.00%, SipTrack will check your medication list and alert you if any of them have a known interaction with alcohol. It also alerts you when your BAC clears to 0.00%.

---

**Q7: Is SipTrack free?**
The core app is free: real-time BAC tracking, drink logging, 7-day session history, and Apple Watch support. SipTrack Plus ($4.99/month or $39.99/year) unlocks unlimited history, the full medication database, custom drink volumes, and additional export formats.

---

**Q8: What is the legal limit?**
In most US states, the legal limit for driving is a BAC of 0.08%. In some states and for commercial drivers, the limit is lower (0.04%). Many countries use different limits. SipTrack defaults to 0.08% and allows you to set a personal limit that's lower. SipTrack always recommends erring on the side of caution — the legal limit is a legal threshold, not a safety guarantee.
