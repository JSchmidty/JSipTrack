### Description
*(4000 characters max)*

**SipTrack** tells you your estimated BAC (Blood Alcohol Content — the percentage of alcohol in your bloodstream) in real time, using the clinically validated Widmark formula. Input your drinks as you go. SipTrack does the math.

Whether you track for safety, health, medication awareness, recovery, or just curiosity — SipTrack gives you clear data and lets you decide what to do with it.

**YOUR REAL-TIME DASHBOARD**

A large, clean gauge shows your estimated BAC at a glance. Color shifts from green (clear) to amber (approaching your limit) to red (over the legal limit of 0.08% in most of the US). Log any drink in under 5 seconds. See your safe-to-drive estimated time update instantly.

**WHAT SIPTRACK TRACKS**

• Real-time BAC estimate — updated as your body metabolizes alcohol
• Safe-to-drive countdown — know exactly when you'll be clear
• Drink count and session duration
• Peak BAC for each session
• Drink-free day streaks
• Session history and weekly trends
• Apple Watch complications — check your number from your wrist

**MODES FOR EVERY SITUATION**

SipTrack includes purpose-built modes:

Standard Mode — full BAC tracking with all features
Discreet Mode — a minimal widget that shows only a number; no context visible to others around you
Recovery Mode — streak counter and day tracking, with BAC gauge hidden if you prefer
Professional Tasting Mode — log small samples with precise volumes; designed for sommeliers and review professionals
Designated Driver Mode — shows a single "I'm the driver" badge; no BAC display

**SAFETY FIRST**

Emergency SOS — one tap sends your location and a brief message to an emergency contact
Rideshare link — get a ride when you need one, directly from the app
Medication warnings — enter your medications and SipTrack alerts you when your BAC may affect them
Hydration reminders — optional check-ins to pace yourself

**COMPLETELY PRIVATE**

Every drink you log stays on your device. SipTrack uses on-device SQLite storage. No data is sent to SipTech servers. We do not know what you log, when you log it, or how much. Your data is yours.

Export your session history as a CSV file at any time — useful for medical appointments, personal tracking, or simply keeping records.

**DESIGNED FOR EVERYONE**

SipTrack was designed and tested across 25 user personas, ages 19 to 71, including health trackers, people in recovery, medical professionals, family members, and college students. WCAG AAA accessibility standards. Full Dynamic Type support. VoiceOver compatible.

**IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER**

SipTrack's BAC estimates are calculated using the Widmark formula and are approximations only. Individual metabolism, medications, food intake, and health conditions significantly affect actual BAC. Do not use this estimate to decide whether to drive. Always err on the side of caution. SipTrack is not a medical device.

### Age Rating Justification

**Recommended Rating: 17+ (Frequent/Intense References to Alcohol or Tobacco)**

**Rationale:**
SipTrack is a BAC tracking tool. While it does not glamorize or encourage drinking, its core function references alcohol use. An age rating of 17+ is appropriate because:
1. Drinking is generally not legal for users under 21 in the United States (under 18 in many international markets)
2. The app includes an age verification gate to discourage underage access
3. This rating is consistent with competitor health/tracking apps in the alcohol awareness category
4. A 17+ rating does not significantly restrict the target audience, as our youngest validated persona is 19

We recommend against a 4+ or 12+ rating, as it may create regulatory concerns and brand trust issues with medical and professional users.
